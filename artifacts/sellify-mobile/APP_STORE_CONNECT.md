## Full beskrivning

### Svenska

Sellify är marknadsplatsen där det går snabbt att sälja det du inte längre använder.

SÅ FUNKAR DET
• Fota eller välj bilder på din vara
• AI:n analyserar bilderna och skriver titel, beskrivning och prisförslag
• Berätta mer med rösten eller texten – annonsen uppdateras direkt
• Granska, justera och publicera

FLER FUNKTIONER
• Sök och filtrera bland annonser i hela landet
• Chatta direkt med köpare och säljare i appen
• Pushnotiser när du får nya meddelanden
• Spara favoriter
• Varje annons får en egen webbsida som du kan dela var som helst
• Skapa snabbt en liknande annons av något du redan sålt
• Appen finns på svenska och engelska

Sellify är gratis att använda. Affären gör ni upp direkt mellan er – utan avgifter.

### Engelska

Sellify is the marketplace that makes selling what you no longer use fast and easy.

HOW IT WORKS
• Snap or pick photos of your item
• AI analyzes the photos and writes the title, description and a price suggestion
• Add details with your voice or by typing – the listing updates instantly
• Review, adjust and publish

MORE FEATURES
• Search and filter listings across the country
• Chat directly with buyers and sellers in the app
• Push notifications for new messages
• Save favorites
• Every listing gets its own web page you can share anywhere
• Quickly create a similar listing from something you already sold
• Available in Swedish and English

Sellify is free to use. Buyers and sellers deal directly with each other – no fees.

```
Sellify is a second-hand marketplace for private individuals.

HOW TO TEST
1. Sign in with the demo account below (or create a new account with any email).
2. Browse listings on the Home tab — no account is needed for browsing.
3. To test the AI listing flow: go to the "Sälj"/"Sell" tab, take or pick a photo
   of any object, and the AI will generate a title, description and price
   suggestion. You can add details by typing (send button) or by voice (microphone
   button). Review and publish.
4. Each published listing gets a public web page (share button on the listing).
5. Messaging: open any listing and contact the seller; push notifications are
   sent for new messages.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile tab -> "Delete account" -> confirmation dialog. This permanently deletes
the account, listings, messages and favorites (server-side, including the auth
provider record).

PERMISSIONS
- Camera / Photo library: used to photograph or pick product images for listings.
- Microphone: optional; the user can describe the item by voice, the audio is
  transcribed server-side to improve the listing draft, and is not stored.
- Location (when in use): optional; used only to pre-fill the user's city in a
  new listing. Exact location is never stored or shown.
- Push notifications: optional; used for new-message alerts.

Sign in with Apple and Google are offered alongside email/password.
No payments are processed in the app; buyers and sellers transact directly.
```

## Övrigt

- **Åldersgräns (Age rating):** svara "No" på allt utom "Unrestricted Web Access"
  → normalt 4+ eller 12+ beroende på svar om användargenererat innehåll. Appen
  har användargenererat innehåll (annonser + chatt) – ange att innehåll kan
  rapporteras/tas bort via kontakt, och att konton kan blockeras vid missbruk.
- **Priser:** Gratis, inga köp i appen.
- **ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption:** redan satt till `false` i appen — inga
  extra frågor om export compliance vid varje build.
